Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 404 Media's content automation tool (the "Service") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect a TikTok account to publish content automatically.

1. Information We Collect

  • TikTok account information: your TikTok user ID (open_id), display name, and profile information made available via TikTok's API after you authorize the connection.
  • Access tokens: OAuth tokens issued by TikTok that allow the Service to publish content on your behalf. These are stored securely and used only for the scopes you authorize.
  • Content data: video files, captions, and posting metadata submitted through the Service for publishing to TikTok.
  • Usage data: basic logs of posting activity (timestamps, success/failure status) used for troubleshooting.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to operate the Service: authenticating your TikTok account, publishing authorized content via TikTok's Content Posting API, and maintaining a record of what was posted and when.

3. What We Don't Do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your TikTok data for advertising or profiling unrelated to the Service.
  • We do not access TikTok data beyond the scopes you explicitly authorize.

4. Data Retention

Access tokens and account information are retained only as long as your TikTok account remains connected to the Service. You may revoke access at any time, after which we delete stored tokens and associated account data within a reasonable period.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect stored tokens and content data, including encrypted storage and restricted access.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke the Service's access to your TikTok account at any time via TikTok's app permissions settings. You may also request deletion of any data we hold about you by contacting us.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
