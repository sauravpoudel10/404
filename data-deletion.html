Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 11, 2026

404 Media's content automation tool (the "Service") publishes content to social media accounts that its operator owns. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect personal data from members of the public.

What the Service stores

  • Access tokens for the operator's own Facebook Page, Instagram account, X account and TikTok account, used only to publish content.
  • A record of published posts — the post IDs, timestamps and captions of content the Service itself created.
  • Generated media — the images and short videos the Service produced, which are deleted automatically 48 hours after publication.

No data about people who view, like, comment on or otherwise interact with published content is collected or stored.

How to request deletion

If you believe the Service holds data relating to you, email sauravpaudel532@gmail.com with the subject line Data deletion request and a description of the data concerned. Requests are actioned within 30 days and confirmed by reply.

Removing the Service's access

The operator can revoke the Service's access at any time:

  1. Facebook and Instagram: in Meta Business Settings, remove the Service's system user or delete its access token. Any stored token becomes invalid immediately.
  2. TikTok: in TikTok settings, under Security & permissions → Apps and services, remove the Service. Stored tokens are then invalid.
  3. X: in X settings, under Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps, revoke the Service.

Content already published remains on the respective platform under the operator's account until removed there.
